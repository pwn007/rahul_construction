## 2. Audit — Existing Site (archubicbuildcon.com)

### 2.1 Information architecture
`/` · `/about` · `/services` · `/services/mep` · `/projects` · `/pricing` · `/calculator` · `/contact` — 8 routes.

### 2.2 What works (keep & elevate)
| Feature | Why it works | Our upgrade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cost Calculator** in primary nav | Lead magnet with real intent capture | Rebuilt as a 6-step cinematic wizard with live cost model + PDF |
| Dual pricing models (labour vs turnkey) | Serves both self-managing and hands-off buyers | Interactive comparison + "which is right for me?" chooser |
| MEPF as a first-class page | Genuine technical differentiator | Full service hub with 4 sub-disciplines |
| "Zero chaos" differentiator framing | Emotional, memorable | Elevated to a scroll-told narrative section |
| Process (5 stages) | Reduces perceived risk | Interactive pinned timeline, 6 stages incl. free maintenance |

### 2.3 What fails (fix)
- **No testimonials anywhere** despite having four strong 5★ reviews in the portfolio.
- **No project detail pages.** Projects are cards that go nowhere — the single biggest credibility leak.
- **No blog / careers / gallery / downloads.** Zero SEO surface, zero talent funnel.
- Calculator's 13 material-brand pickers are **cognitive overload before value** — it asks for TMT
  steel brand before it has told you anything. Inverted funnel.
- Calculator output is a flat 3-line number. No breakdown by construction head, no timeline,
  no downloadable artifact, no follow-up hook.
- Statistics ("500+ projects, 15+ years") conflict with the portfolio PDF (80+, 30+ combined).
  **We use the PDF numbers — the source of truth.**
- No client-facing project tracking despite "Project Management & Tracking" being a listed service.
- Generic stock-feel imagery; no art direction system.

## 3. Audit — Competitor (reidiusinfra.in)

### 3.1 IA
`index` · `about` · `architecture` (all projects) · `interior` · `residences` · `commercial` ·
`careers` · `contact` · `blog` (Learn) · `calculator` · `privacy-policy` · `terms`

### 3.2 What they do better than the client
- **Careers page** → positions them as an employer of scale.
- **"Learn" blog** → top-of-funnel SEO and authority.
- **16-person named leadership grid** with photos and titles → enormous trust signal.
- **Safety & compliance section** → speaks to commercial buyers, not just homeowners.
- **Portfolio PDF download** repeated 3× per page → low-friction lead capture.
- **WhatsApp booking deep link** → matches actual Indian buyer behaviour.
- **"120-day construction guarantee"** → a hard, checkable promise. Fear-reversal.
- Cost calculator badged **NEW** in nav → merchandises the tool.

### 3.3 What we improve on
- Their calculator front-loads brand selection (same flaw as Archubic) and outputs a raw rupee
  wall with formatting bugs (`₹100,46,18,760.00`). We ship correct Indian-locale formatting
  (lakh/crore grouping) and a *visual* breakdown.
- Their leadership grid is a flat static list — we make it an editorial, filterable team section.
- Their project pages are galleries without narrative — we ship real case studies with
  challenge → approach → outcome, specs, timeline, before/after.
- No client portal. No live site monitoring surface (the client's camera-monitoring USP has
  **no digital expression anywhere in this market** — we build it).

## 4. Global Benchmark Research

| Firm | Lesson extracted |
| --- | --- |
| **Turner Construction** | Projects are the primary content type, not capabilities. Filterable by market / region / year / client type; every project gets its own page with photography, scope and the people behind it. |
| **Skanska USA** | Segments the homepage into distinct buyer journeys (Clients · Investors · Talent) instead of one crowded page. |
| **DPR Construction** | Technology and method as brand — BIM/process shown as proof of rigour. |
| **Bechtel / Fluor / Kiewit** | Scale communicated through data density and restraint, never through adjectives. |
| **Foster + Partners / Gensler** | Editorial art direction; whitespace as luxury; imagery does the persuading. |
| **Bouygues / VINCI** | Sustainability and safety as structured, measurable commitments. |

### 4.1 Motion & craft benchmark (Awwwards / FWA / CSSDA, 2026)
- **Production stack:** Lenis smooth scroll + GSAP ScrollTrigger + Framer Motion; WebGL only where it earns its weight.
- **Dominant patterns:** section pinning with chaptered narrative, scroll-driven image sequences,
  weighted inertia scroll that makes the page feel like "one continuous surface", magnetic cursor
  affordances, mask/clip-path reveals, kinetic typography, view-transition page changes.
- **Anti-patterns to avoid:** 4-second loaders with no content, scroll-jacking that breaks
  keyboard/screen-reader flow, animation on every element (fatigue), heavy 3D on mobile.

### 4.2 Our motion doctrine
> **Motion must carry meaning: reveal hierarchy, express material, or confirm an action.
> Anything else is deleted.** Everything respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Nothing blocks
> content for more than 900 ms. Mobile gets a reduced motion budget by default.
