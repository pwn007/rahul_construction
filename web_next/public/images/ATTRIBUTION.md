# Image attribution

All imagery in `public/images` is curated from **Wikimedia Commons**, including its
Unsplash CC0 imports — with two exceptions — `mepf-09.webp` from Unsplash direct and `mepf-10.webp` from Pexels. Files
were downsized to 1500 px and recompressed for the web, and are served from this project’s
own origin (no hotlinking).

> **Before commercial launch:** verify each licence. CC BY and CC BY-SA require visible
> attribution; CC0 and public-domain files do not. Replacing these with the client’s own
> project photography removes the obligation entirely.

> **The `mepf-*` block is the one that needs attention.** Every other pool is CC0. All seven
> sprinkler photos (`mepf-01`–`mepf-07`) are CC BY or CC BY-SA, which require attribution the
> reader can see — and no photograph on this site currently renders a credit anywhere. Two of
> them ship today: `mepf-07` on `/services/mepf-consultancy`, `mepf-05` on the MEPF blog post.
> Either add a visible credit, or replace those two the way `mepf-09` was sourced.
>
> `mepf-01`, `-02`, `-03`, `-04` and `-06` are in the pool but no seed resolves to them, so

## Geospatial data

The Project Atlas draws Jaipur's real municipal zone boundaries. The geometry is
not an image and does not live in this folder — it is vendored at
`web/scripts/data/jaipur-zones.geojson` and compiled into
`src/data/jaipur-districts.geometry.ts` by `npm run build:districts`.

| Source | Source title | Licence | Author |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `scripts/data/jaipur-zones.geojson` | [Municipal Spatial Data — Jaipur zones](https://github.com/datameet/Municipal_Spatial_Data) | CC BY 4.0 | DataMeet |

**This one is already discharged, and must stay that way.** Unlike the photographs
above — which are mostly CC0 and carry no on-page obligation — CC BY 4.0 requires
attribution the reader can actually see. The `ProjectAtlas` component renders that
credit beneath every map from `ATLAS_SOURCE`. Removing it to tidy the layout would
put the site out of licence compliance, so it is not a styling decision.

Full provenance, the sha256 the build pins, and the boundary-vintage caveat are in
`web/scripts/data/LICENCE-datameet.txt`.

## Swapping in the client’s own photography

Every image resolves through `src/lib/media.ts`. Drop real photos into `public/images`
using the same pool naming (`residential-01.jpg`, `interior-03.jpg` …) and regenerate
`src/data/image-catalogue.ts`. **No component changes are required.**
