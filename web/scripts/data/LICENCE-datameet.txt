VINTAGE — READ BEFORE TRUSTING THESE AS CURRENT BOUNDARIES
----------------------------------------------------------
These are the *pre-2019* unified Jaipur Municipal Corporation zones. In
December 2020 the JMC was split into JMC-Heritage (100 wards) and JMC-Greater
(150 wards), and these polygons do not reflect that. No free vector data for
the post-split boundaries could be found.

For a marketing map this is acceptable, and arguably preferable — the older
zone names are the ones Jaipur residents actually use. It is NOT acceptable
for anything asserting current administrative fact. The atlas panel carries a
provenance line saying so.

COVERAGE LIMIT
--------------
These zones cover the old JMC area only. Land developed by the Jaipur
Development Authority beyond that limit — Pratap Nagar most notably — falls
outside every polygon. That is a true fact about the city, not a data defect,
and the atlas renders such projects on the out-of-boundary ground with an
explicit note rather than snapping them into the nearest zone.
